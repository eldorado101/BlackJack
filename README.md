# Blackjack

A simple, multiplayer Blackjack game built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Play in your browser with 2 human players, 2 AI opponents, and 1 dealer. Zero dependencies, works offline.



How to Play
1. Click **New Game** to deal cards.
2. Player 1 & 2 choose `Hit` or `Stand`.
3. AI players take their turns automatically.
4. Dealer reveals cards and finishes the round.
5. Results appear instantly. Click **Play Again** to restart.

 Run Locally
- Download or clone this repo.
- Double-click `index.html` to open in any browser.
- No installs, servers, or build steps required.
